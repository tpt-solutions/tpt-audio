## Hero

**tpt-audio — route any sound, anywhere.**

A simple, modern audio router and virtual mixer with a visual routing matrix and
per-app volume control. Zero-latency, low CPU, no audio expertise required.

## Subhead

Route a game's audio to your stream mic, send music to a second output, or mute a noisy
app — all from one clean grid. Works on Windows, Linux (PipeWire), and Archon.

## Feature bullets

- **Visual routing matrix** — sources on the left, sinks across the top, click to connect.
- **Per-route gain & mute** — fine-tune every connection with a slider.
- **Per-app volume** — control any application's output independently.
- **Presets you can share** — save, load, export, and import routing setups as files.
- **Live device list** — devices and apps appear automatically; hotplug safe.
- **Diagnostics built in** — catch dropouts with underrun/overrun counters and an event log.

## Download section

- **Windows** — Download the signed MSI installer. Requires Windows 10/11 (WASAPI).
- **Linux** — Available as a Flatpak (PipeWire required). `.deb` and AppImage coming soon.
- **Archon** — Distributed through the Archon package manager (research-gated).

## "Why tpt-audio?" section

Voicemeeter is powerful but complex and buggy. PulseAudio/JACK setups are a nightmare for
everyday users. tpt-audio gives you the routing power you want behind a grid you already
understand.
